The Journey

How It Started

A quick look at how a college idea turned into a national platform for student-athletes.

2024

The Idea Takes Shape

As a student at Lynn University, Ian founded Bigger Than Sports to help high school athletes build a plan for what comes after the game.

Jul 2024

First Community Tournament

Bigger Than Sports hosted its first 3-on-3 basketball tournament back home in Amherst, MA, turning a hometown idea into a real fundraiser.

Nov 2024

Growing the Mission

A second 3v3 tournament in Boca Raton brought student-athletes and mentors together and expanded the program's reach.

Dec 2024

On the Mic

Ian joined the Modern Creator Podcast to talk about transitioning into purpose-driven work.

Jul 2025

Center Stage at the ESPYs

Ian and Bigger Than Sports were honored with the Billie Jean King Youth Leadership Award, sharing the stage with Damar Hamlin and Billie Jean King.

Oct 2025

National Recognition

Lynn University and outlets across the country spotlighted the work, cementing Bigger Than Sports as a model for youth mentorship.
